## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a futuristic, high-clarity intelligence environment designed for deep sentiment analysis, polarization monitoring, and opinion consensus auditing. Tailored for data scientists, risk analysts, and executive teams, the interface balances ultra-dense analytical utilities with an ethereal, optical aesthetic.

The design movement blends **Rich Glassmorphism** with **Technical High-Contrast Precision**. Heavy, tinted backdrop blurs, luminous ambient radial light leaks, and multi-layered crystalline surfaces float over deep, obsidian-like dark canvas spaces. Translucent peach tiers soften clinical data density, while razor-sharp neon-lime and aggressive crimson signals cut through the glass layers with immediate, high-salience telemetry.

## Colors

The palette operates on high-contrast nocturnal depth, using radiant translucent overlays to simulate light passing through dense crystalline glass.

- **Canvas & Surface Base (`#0D0F12`)**: The void background, accented with faint undertones of deep charcoal and midnight smoke (`#14171D`).
- **Primary / Soft Peach (`#F9EAD7`)**: Used for luminous highlights, high-tier glassy overlays (`rgba(249, 234, 215, 0.08)` to `rgba(249, 234, 215, 0.16)`), primary headers, key metrics, and soft specular highlights along top card edges.
- **Secondary / Lime Green (`#BFE23A`)**: The metric validation engine. Drives consensus indicators, high-confidence scores, positive sentiment trajectories, and verified claim badges. Emits a localized ambient lime glow (`rgba(191, 226, 58, 0.2)`).
- **Tertiary / Crimson Alert (`#E10600`)**: Reserved strictly for high-urgency interventions: the Critical-Minority Safety Guard alert, polarization hazard flags, rapid outlier drops, and active defensive perimeter badges. Emits a sharp, urgent glow (`rgba(225, 6, 0, 0.25)`).
- **Glass Borders & Dividers**: Defined strictly via linear gradients transitioning from `rgba(249, 234, 215, 0.28)` at the light-facing top-left to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` at the shadowed bottom-right.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear analytical authority:
- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Technical, structural, and modernist. Used for overarching dashboard titles, module labels, and primary statistical rollups.
- **Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Soft, humanist, and highly legible against dark and glassy backdrops. Prevents eye strain during long-form opinion feed analysis and narrative summaries.
- **Labels & Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Strict, monospaced tabular data display. All consensus percentage points, risk matrix indices, timestamps, and confidence intervals are locked to this family to preserve alignment across fluctuating real-time streams.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column fluid grid system pinned to a maximum canvas bounds of 1600px to maintain analytical control rooms within standard human visual span. 

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with a 1.25rem gutter and 2rem outer margin. Multi-column glass panels nest side-by-side to expose telemetry graphs, opinion heatmaps, and sentiment logs simultaneously.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns with 1rem gutters and 1.25rem margins. Telemetry panels stack vertically; tertiary status drawers collapse into floating modal sheets.
- **Mobile (&lt;768px)**: 4 columns with 0.75rem gutters and 1rem margins. All cards span full width, with sticky navigation glass docks floating at the screen base.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use drop shadows; depth is rendered entirely through **multi-tiered optical refraction and ambient luminous backlights**.

- **Canvas Tier (0)**: Solid `#0D0F12` augmented with two fixed ambient blur orbs: an ethereal Lime Green node (`rgba(191, 226, 58, 0.08)`, 160px blur) positioned behind consensus clusters, and a deep Crimson node (`rgba(225, 6, 0, 0.07)`, 180px blur) pinned near anomalous sentiment clusters.
- **Base Glass Panels (Tier 1)**: Primary cards and chart modules. Background: `rgba(20, 23, 29, 0.65)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px) saturate(180%)`. Edge: 1px continuous hairline gradient border ranging from `rgba(249, 234, 215, 0.2)` at the light apex to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)` at the bottom.
- **Elevated Interactive Glass (Tier 2)**: Hovered cards, floating tooltips, and flyout filters. Background: `rgba(249, 234, 215, 0.08)` blended over `rgba(13, 15, 18, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(36px)`. Border opacity steps up to `rgba(249, 234, 215, 0.4)`. Box shadow: soft inner glow (`inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(249, 234, 215, 0.25)`).
- **Critical Intercept Glass (Tier 3)**: Modals and Critical-Minority Alert banners. Background: `rgba(225, 6, 0, 0.08)` anchored onto `rgba(18, 10, 11, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(48px)`. Rimmed with a distinct 1px specular stroke of `rgba(225, 6, 0, 0.6)` and an exterior atmospheric crimson halo (`0 0 32px rgba(225, 6, 0, 0.2)`).

## Shapes

The shape system employs rounded geometric forms to temper high-contrast analytical data, balancing raw information density with polished ergonomic surfaces.

- **Micro Badges & Chips**: 0.5rem radius, providing compact, pill-adjacent containers for technical indices.
- **Glass Panels & Metric Containers**: 1rem radius (`rounded-lg`), maximizing inner refraction surface area along curved corners.
- **Overlays & Dialog Sheets**: 1.5rem radius (`rounded-xl`), presenting organic, lens-like silhouettes that emphasize depth over background structures.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Consensus Action)**: High-sheen Soft Peach surface (`#F9EAD7`) with pitch text (`#0D0F12`), Space Grotesk 600 weight. Subtle internal white gloss highlight along top edge. On hover, receives a gentle lime back-glow.
- **Secondary Glass**: Frosted translucent shell (`rgba(249, 234, 215, 0.06)`), 1px border (`rgba(249, 234, 215, 0.2)`), Peach text. On hover, background shifts to `rgba(249, 234, 215, 0.12)`.
- **Critical Action (Guard Intervention)**: Deep translucent crimson background (`rgba(225, 6, 0, 0.15)`), solid crimson border (`#E10600`), vibrant white text with a persistent outer ambient warning pulse.

### Badges & Status Tags
- **Sentiment Consensus Badge**: JetBrains Mono uppercase, 0.5rem radius, micro-padding (`0.25rem 0.6rem`). Bound by an ultra-thin 1px solid border (`#BFE23A`), `rgba(191, 226, 58, 0.12)` fill, and `#BFE23A` text accompanied by a glowing 4px radial status indicator.
- **Critical-Minority Safety Tag**: Saturated Crimson fill (`rgba(225, 6, 0, 0.2)`), `#E10600` typography, framed with high-contrast crimson edge lighting.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Glass trench styling: Recessed translucent background (`rgba(8, 10, 13, 0.7)`), inset shadow (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`), 1px hairline border (`rgba(249, 234, 215, 0.15)`).
- Focus state: Border transforms to `#BFE23A` with an exterior blur glow of 8px at 20% opacity. Placeholder text rendered in muted peach-tinted slate (`rgba(249, 234, 215, 0.4)`).

### Cards & Analytical Containers
- Built using Tier 1 glass specifications. Headers feature an integrated sub-grid displaying the metric label in JetBrains Mono alongside a lime or crimson visual delta chip.
- Card interiors use faint divider lines (`1px solid rgba(249, 234, 215, 0.06)`) to preserve visual continuity without creating harsh visual barriers.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Toggles
- Inactive controls: Frosted dark disks or squares with a faint 1px border (`rgba(249, 234, 215, 0.2)`).
- Active state: Fills with high-luminance Lime Green (`#BFE23A`) with an obsidian micro-checkmark (`#0D0F12`).
- Track switches: Deep translucent capsules with floating Soft Peach toggle thumbs (`#F9EAD7`) providing clear physical affordance.

### Critical-Minority Safety Guard Banner
- A dedicated marquee component mounted at the top level of volatile viewpoints. Glass panel with an internal gradient tint from `rgba(225, 6, 0, 0.25)` to transparent. Left edge anchored by a 4px solid Crimson spine. Displays live divergence tracking with monospaced outlier indexes.
